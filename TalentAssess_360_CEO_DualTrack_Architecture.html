⚖️ Client-Facing Track vs. Internal Enterprise Track

How the platform cleanly isolates both operational demands while utilizing shared AI and proctoring engines.

Commercial Stream

🌐 Client-Facing Recruitment Portal

  • Purpose: Fulfilling external client client contracts, staffing mandates, and dedicated client hiring drives.
  • Customization: Client-specific test sets, tailored difficulty cutoffs, and client branding headers in test links.
  • Evaluation Matrix: Automatically outputs formatted executive PDF profiles and scorecards to share with client hiring managers.
  • Revenue Impact: Enables Intelligenz IT to act as an automated high-margin Recruitment-as-a-Service (RaaS) partner.
Internal Stream

🏢 Intelligenz IT Internal Hiring Portal

  • Purpose: Sourcing and onboarding core engineering, AI R&D, product managers, and leadership teams for Intelligenz IT.
  • Rigorous Testing: Multi-tiered general aptitude (GT) + hands-on technical coding (TT) with zero tolerance for cheating.
  • Recruiter Dashboard: Integrated inside existing `Manage Candidate` with 1-click candidate invites and custom WYSIWYG email templates.
  • Company Impact: Eliminates agency commissions and ensures top 1% technical talent entering our payroll.
